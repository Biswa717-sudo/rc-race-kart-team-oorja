4. POWER DISTRIBUTION & WIRING RULES (CRITICAL FOR HARDWARE SAFETY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Dual 5V BEC Split (DO NOT tie the two red 5V ESC wires together):
   - ESC #1 Red (+5V)  --> Powers ESP32 (VIN/5V pin) + 4-Channel Relay Board (VCC).
                           Keep the JD-VCC / VCC jumper cap INSTALLED on relay board.
   - ESC #2 Red (+5V)  --> Powers Steering Servo (+5V) + RC Receiver (if used).
   - Common Ground     --> Connect Black GND wires of ESC #1, ESC #2, ESP32,
                           Relay Board, and Servo all together.

B. Safe ESP32 GPIO Pin Selection:
   - Use safe pins: GPIO 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 33.
   - AVOID strapping pins (GPIO 0, 2, 12, 15) for relays to prevent boot glitches.

C. Relay Phase-Swapper Wiring (Repeat for Motor 1 on Relays 1-2 & Motor 2 on Relays 3-4):
   - ESC Wire 3  --> Direct to Motor Wire 3 (no relay)
   - Relay A COM --> Motor Wire 1 | NC --> ESC Wire 1 | NO --> ESC Wire 2
   - Relay B COM --> Motor Wire 2 | NC --> ESC Wire 2 | NO --> ESC Wire 1

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GOLDEN SAFETY RULES & EMPIRICAL MOTOR STOP-TIME CALIBRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MANDATORY STOP-TIME TEST:
   Before driving, empirically measure how long your A2212 motors take to come to
   a dead stop from 100% throttle.
   - Formula: Firmware Delay = (Measured Stop Time x 1.30) + 50ms contact settle.
   - Typical with SimonK Brake ON: ~150ms-220ms stop time -> set delay to 300ms-350ms.
   - Typical with SimonK Brake OFF: ~600ms-900ms stop time -> set delay to 850ms-1200ms.
   - Test Method: Record high-speed 120fps video of the spinning motor with white tape,
     or use the ESP32 serial calibration sketch to measure exact milliseconds.

2. NEVER switch relays while throttle > 1000us or while motor is still spinning!
   Always drop ESC signal to 1000us (0% throttle), wait the calibrated dead-time,
   toggle relays, wait 50ms, then apply reverse throttle.
3. Enable SimonK built-in brake on both ESCs via the throttle calibration beep menu.
   This drops spin-down time by over 60%, protecting relays and speeding up recovery.
4. Insulate all exposed solder joints and the bottom of the relay board with heat
   shrink or electrical tape so chassis parts cannot cause a short circuit.
5. Mount the relay board on vibration-damping foam tape to prevent contact chatter
   on the 2-5mm redstone tile seams.
6. Double-check battery plug type (XT60 vs Deans T-plug) before ordering adapters.
